================================================================================
                    YOUTOK - MASTER REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
Platform: Android TV (Amlogic S905X, Android 7.1.2 Nougat, 1GB RAM)
Architecture: Single Universal APK (armeabi-v7a 32-bit + arm64-v8a 64-bit)
Playback Core: SmartTube MediaServiceCore + ExoPlayer (100% Unaltered)
UI & Behavior: Custom TikTok-Style Continuous TV Experience, YouTube Red (#E50914)
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PLAYBACK ENGINE & CORE GUARANTEE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Keep SmartTube's video loading and playback engine (MediaServiceCore + custom 
  ExoPlayer) 100% intact and unchanged.
- Must solve live YouTube n-sig JS cipher and poToken attestation without fail.
- Absolutely NO video playback errors, NO title-only loading, and NO skipping to 
  the next video in an infinite loop.
- Deliver ONE single signed universal APK ("YouTok.apk") supporting both 32-bit 
  (armeabi-v7a) and 64-bit (arm64-v8a).
- Must eliminate the "Package appears to be invalid" / parse error on Android 7.1.2.
- Direct APK download link on GitHub Releases (never zipped).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BRANDING & VISUAL IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- App Name: YouTok (no "beta" suffix or other names).
- Accent Color: YouTube Red (#E50914) with pitch-black / dark glass backgrounds.
- App Icons & Banners: Dark squircle icon (#181818) with red badge and bold "Y" 
  emblem, plus a 320x180 Leanback launcher banner.
- Modern Loading Screen: Clean rotating circular spinner.
- No Obstructive Text: ZERO text during loading (no "Searching for next video...",
  no "Please wait while data is loading...").

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. TIKTOK-STYLE TV FEED & SINGLE VIEW MODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Continuous Vertical Feed: Videos play full-screen automatically. Pressing D-Pad 
  DOWN advances to the next video, and D-Pad UP goes to the previous video.
- Single View Mode Only: NO toggling between normal mode and full screen mode.
- 3-Second Auto-Hide Overlay: Video title, channel info, progress bar, and 
  playback controls show for exactly 3 seconds on launch, seek, or play/pause, 
  and then automatically fade out (even while paused).
- Stop Background Searching on Leaving Feed: If searching for the next video in 
  the background, stop searching when leaving Home; resume searching only when 
  returning to Home.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. REMOTE CONTROL BUTTON BINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- D-Pad UP:    Smoothly swipe to previous video (TikTok style).
- D-Pad DOWN:  Smoothly swipe to next video (TikTok style).
- D-Pad LEFT:  Seek backward (-10s / -5s); displays timeline overlay for 3s.
- D-Pad RIGHT: Seek forward (+10s / +5s); displays timeline overlay for 3s.
- OK / CENTER: Toggle Play / Pause; displays overlay for 3s.
- POWER (Hold): Does NOTHING. Holding or repeating KEYCODE_POWER is consumed by 
               the app so playback and TV viewing are never interrupted.
- BACK (Single): Returns to previous screen / shelf / Home feed.
- BACK (from Search Video): Pressing Back on a video opened from Search returns 
               directly to the Search results screen with keyboard ready.
- BACK (Double-Press on Home): Pauses video and shows Exit Confirmation Dialog.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. AUTO-PAUSE & RESUME NAVIGATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Exit Dialog: Double-pressing Back pauses playback. If Cancel or Back is 
  pressed, playback resumes. If Confirm is selected, the app closes.
- Leaving Home: Navigating away from Home to Search, History, or Settings 
  automatically pauses playback.
- Returning to Home: Returning to Home automatically resumes video playback.
- Search Auto-Keyboard: Entering Search automatically pops up the on-screen 
  Leanback TV keyboard.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. MINIMAL, BLOAT-FREE UI & SETTINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Strip All SmartTube Bloat: Remove Shorts shelf, Trending, Kids Home, Gaming, 
  News, Music, Sports, Live, Channels, Notifications, Playlists, Blocked Channels.
- 4 Essential Sidebar Sections Only:
    1. Home (continuous video feed)
    2. Subscriptions (followed channels)
    3. History (watch history)
    4. Settings (essential options)
- Streamlined Settings Menu (Normal Users Only):
    1. Accounts (Sign in / Sign out with QR or code)
    2. Video Player (Quality / Resolution presets)
    3. Language & Country (Content region)
    4. Subtitles (Language & styling)
    5. Remote Control / Cast (Link TV code)
    6. About YouTok (Clean app details)
- Remove developer options, deep SponsorBlock settings, AFR toggles, and 
  backup/restore menus.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. WATCH HISTORY MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Clean vertical list layout for watched videos.
- Single-Item Delete Button: Every item features an individual delete action 
  with a trash can icon ("🗑️ Remove from history").
- Delete All History with 10s Countdown: Selecting "Clear History" displays a 
  confirmation dialog with a visible 10-second countdown before clearing, 
  with a Cancel option to abort at any time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
8. TIPS & SHORTCUTS (NO TOASTS, IN-MENU CAROUSEL)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- No Floating Toasts: Completely remove on-screen toasts ("Press again to exit", 
  "Hold to refresh", etc.).
- In-Menu Tips Drawer: All tips are stored neatly inside the menu box.
- Left/Right Carousel: Displays one tip at a time; pressing Left or Right on the 
  remote switches to the previous or next tip cleanly.
